# Ideas

These are plans for directions PLEASAND could take next. Each one was written, reviewed by an evaluator that scored it out of 10 on consistency (does it hold together with the code and with itself), design (is it a good rule for this world) and entertainment (is it fun to watch and to play with), then revised and reviewed again. The target was an average of 8.5. A different plan, the hand, reached it first and is the one being built (see `docs/the-hand-plan.md`). None of these is built. Each is kept as it stood when the work stopped, latest revision included, so it can be picked up later. Each file opens with its scores, and "best" below is the highest average any version got.

- [Lime and chokedamp](lime-and-chokedamp.md) (v3): a chalk rock you can paint that burns to quicklime, slakes back with water and heat, and breathes off a heavy damp that pools in mines and low places, which the village learns to get out of. Best average 8.17.
- [Daily Bread](daily-bread.md) (v2): wheat ground to flour, wetted to dough and baked into loaves in a brick bakehouse with a quern and an oven heated by real conducted heat, kept below 220 by a baker. Best average 8.17.
- [Pond life](pond-life.md) (v3): weed and fish living in ponds, grazed by ducks, fished from a jetty, and carried from pond to pond by accident in the brigade's buckets. Best average 7.67.
- [Pressure](steam-pressure.md) (v2): steam shut in a pocket builds pressure, rattles, strains and bursts its walls, and a safety valve lets it out by rule. Best average 7.67.
