## Idea bullets

- **The theatre**: comes of fireworks, masonry, a village of six or more, and a few things worth telling. A
  plank stage two up on a stone plinth, a backdrop, red curtains (one hung full at the back, one tied back to the
  post at the front), a painted cloth of a hill and a moon between them, and a lantern at either end of the beam.
  In front of it, three tiers of stone seats, cut into a slope if there is one and built up out of stone if
  there is not. Every so often the boredest of them picks the best tale nobody has acted yet, two of them (three,
  in a big village) play it, and the idle sit on the tiers and watch, and clap. Whoever it happened to plays
  themselves if they are about, or says from the seats that it was not like that
- **The cathedral**: comes of coloured glass, the bell, and a cross weathered away to nothing; whoever was at the
  graveyard when it went thinks of it. A stone nave seventeen wide with a door at either end, pews, three candles
  hung from the vault, a rose window of coloured glass in the gable (green and amber, and ruby at the heart if
  there was gold), a belfry, and a spire thirty-five up with a lightning rod and a weathercock on it. The bell is
  taken down from wherever it hung and rehung in the belfry. Under it all is a crypt, a ladder down through the
  floor, with a niche and a candle in the wall for every name the village remembers: the ones whose crosses
  weathered away come back. The bored go down with a candle and say hello. Every so often the bell rings for a
  service: the idle file into the pews, and the eldest names the dead, and how ("for Gary, who came back from
  space too fast"), and everybody goes home thinking twice as fast for a while. The candles light the rose window
  from behind, the only coloured light in the valley. The rod takes a bolt that was going to hit anything of
  theirs within ten columns of it, and only that. Water that gets into the crypt is bailed out, a pail at a time,
  up the ladder and out onto lower ground, or into the pond. Far from home when the fireworks go off, it is where
  they hide

## Things to try

- Strike lightning on a house beside the cathedral. Then on the open ground past it
- Pour water into the crypt, and watch somebody bail it out, a pail at a time, up the ladder
- Set the rose window alight with something very hot. When it has cooled it is plain glass. Wait
- Let a graveyard fill up, and see whose name comes back
- Set fire to the pews. The stone stands
